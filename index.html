Editorial Profile

I’m Osama Badran — publishing academically as Osama B. Elnezamy — a Physical Therapy Intern holding a B.Sc. in Physical Therapy with Honors (3.92 GPA) from Pharos University in Alexandria.

My work sits at the intersection of bedside care, rigorous research, and analytical clarity. I combine hands-on orthopedic & neurological rehabilitation with clinical research & evidence synthesis (systematic reviews and meta-analyses), healthcare data analytics using Excel, Power BI, DAX, SQL, and R, and visual rehab education through O.B.E.™ MedComics and clinical mind maps.
